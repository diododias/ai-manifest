# Agenda Script — <feature / epic>

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Participants:** <you>, <PM>, <others>
- **Demand:** <1 line>
- **Recording:** <link>

> Fill in whatever you can before the agenda. During the meeting, use it as an agenda. O
> the objective is to force the context that "everyone already knows" to be made explicit — that's what
> does not enter the transcription and undermines the assertiveness of the extraction.

## 0. Context out loud (even if obvious — becomes the anchor of the transcription)
> What is it, which epic, why now.

## 1. Recap of what has already been decided (captures the implicit context of previous agendas)
> "Last time we closed X, Y. Today is about Z."

## 2. Business problem
> What pain, for who, and the **success metric WITH number** (target). Becomes SC-XX.
> Vague adjective ("fast", "better") without a number returns as a gap.

## 3. Main flow (happy path)
> User walkthrough. Who does what. State the **priority** of each piece
> (what is MVP/P1 vs. increment/P2-P3).

## 4. Business rules ← say "business rule:" before each one
> Each rule WITH a concrete/numeric example (the example is what the AI does not infer) and
> WITH the structure: the precondition/trigger ("when…", "while…", "if…") + what
> **the system should** do it. Clear structure = facilitates standardization without inventing clauses.

## 5. Exceptions / edge cases
> Error, limits, invalid states.

## 6. Out of scope
> Which is explicitly NOT supposed to be done.

## 7. Acceptance scenarios ← for each story, say **Given / When / Then**
> The "Then" is the observable result that the AI does not infer. Include the path
> exception (invalid email, empty queue, competition). Ex: "Given month is exhausted,
> When the customer enters the list, they then see confirmation and stay in the queue".

## 8. Open questions
> What was left unanswered — who decides, until when.

## 9. Support materials
> Links, mockups, docs that the PM brought (or are missing).
