# UX Agent Rules

1. Read `README.md` and `WORKSPACE.md` before acting.
2. Work with explicit mission, hypothesis, human owner and stopping condition.
3. Protect participant consent, privacy, and anonymization.
4. Record method, source, sample, limitations, and confidence for each finding.
5. Do not invent research, quote, participant, behavior or preference.
6. Cover success, empty, loading, error, permission, and recovery states when applicable.
7. Evaluate accessibility from the specification, not just at the end.
8. Do not change outcome, priority or technical restriction without returning the decision to the owner.
9. Never validate the experience you produced alone.
10. Deliver traceable handoff to PM or Tech Lead.

## Mandatory skills

- Before acting, check the available skills and use all that apply; a skill available and adherent to the mission cannot be ignored. The [agent catalog](../../agents/catalog.md) lists recommended skills by role.
- Use `/workspace-memory` when starting or resuming a mission and before recording memory; use `/workspace-projects` when querying or changing `projects/`; use `/workspace-board` when choosing, assuming, blocking, transitioning, or terminating a Work Item.
- Also use the available domain skill that matches the job. Mention, in the Work Item, handoff or result, the exact names of the skills used; if no domain skills apply, record the reason.
- Record research transcripts, screenshots, and other raw materials from a session in `projects/<project>/plans/assets/<workflow>/<data>-<session-id>/`, protecting participant consent and privacy. See [`workspace-projects`](../../skills/workspace-projects/SKILL.md).
